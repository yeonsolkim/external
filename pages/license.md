## Giving credit

A credit names the note, the author, the source and the license, with links where possible. For example:

> "Perfect Sets" by Yeonsol Kim, from *External*, licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

## Citing a note

To cite a note in a paper or thesis, treat it as a web page: give the author, title, site, address and the date you accessed it. In BibTeX, for example:

```
@misc{kim-perfect-sets,
  author       = {Kim, Yeonsol},
  title        = {Perfect Sets},
  howpublished = {External, \url{https://yeonsolkim.com/...}},
  year         = {2026},
  note         = {Accessed 6 October 2026}
}
```
