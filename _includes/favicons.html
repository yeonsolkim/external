{%- comment -%} The favicons, in assets/favicons/, served as GitHub serves its own: favicon.svg, the E of New Computer Modern in the weight of the body text (its outline taken from NewCM10-Book), black on a white square, and favicon-dark.svg, the same inverted, white on black, each with a 96-px PNG for browsers that take a PNG over an SVG. The PNG is an icon in its own right, not an alternate, and larger than 48 px, since Google Search reads a site's favicon from its home page, takes no SVG, accepts only the rels icon, shortcut icon and the two apple-touch-icons, and recommends more than 48 px. The script below points both links at the dark pair while the system is in dark mode, at once and whenever the scheme changes, as GitHub's does. The E is centred, three quarters of the square tall (24 of the SVG's 32 px), with its baseline and cap line on whole pixels, and the square's corners are rounded slightly (a radius of 5 of those 32 px). The home-screen icons (apple-touch-icon, which Safari's start page shows too, and the manifest's) draw the same E, centred and 56% of the tile tall, on a white square left square cornered, since iOS and Android cut those to shapes of their own and a maskable icon must fill its tile; 56% keeps the E within that icon's safe zone. apple-mobile-web-app-title names the home-screen icon, which would otherwise take the page's title. {%- endcomment -%}
