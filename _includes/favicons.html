{%- comment -%} The favicons, in assets/favicons/: favicon.svg, the bold E of New Computer Modern, the site's text face (its outline taken from NewCM10-Bold), black on a white square, with a 32-px PNG for browsers that take a PNG over an SVG. Being opaque, the square stands out on a light tab bar and a dark one alike, so it needs no dark-mode pair. The E is centred, 24 of the square's 32 px tall, with its baseline and cap line on whole pixels. The home-screen icons (apple-touch-icon, which Safari's start page shows too, and the manifest's) draw the same E on white, centred and 56% of the tile tall, which keeps it within a maskable icon's safe zone; apple-mobile-web-app-title names the home-screen icon, which would otherwise take the page's title. {%- endcomment -%}
