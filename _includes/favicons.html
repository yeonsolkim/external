{%- comment -%} The favicons, in assets/favicons/: a ∃ after New Computer Modern Math's. In the tab icons it is as tall as the capitals of the tab's title (8.5 of their 16 px) and a little heavier than its letters, its straight edges on whole pixels of a 2x screen. favicon.svg is black on transparent and turns white in dark mode; the PNG and the ICO, for browsers without SVG favicons, stay black. The home-screen icons (apple-touch-icon, the manifest's) are on white, since iOS fills a transparent touch icon with black, and apple-mobile-web-app-title names the home-screen icon, which would otherwise take the page's title. {%- endcomment -%}
