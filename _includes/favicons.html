{%- comment -%} The favicons, in assets/favicons/, served as GitHub serves its own: favicon.svg, a black ∃ on transparent, and favicon-dark.svg, a white one, each with a 32-px PNG for browsers that take a PNG over an SVG. The script below points both links at the dark pair while the system is in dark mode, at once and whenever the scheme changes, as GitHub's does, so no browser shows a dark ∃ on a dark tab bar (Safari would back one with a plate of its own). The ∃ is square cornered, in the proportions of New Computer Modern Math's, as tall as the capitals of Safari's tab titles (19 of its 32 px; Chrome's are 17), a little heavier than their letters, and kept to the right of its 32 px, so that it stands as near its title as GitHub's octocat does. The home-screen icons (apple-touch-icon, which Safari's start page shows too, and the manifest's) draw the same ∃, centred and about 56% of the tile tall, on white, since iOS fills a transparent touch icon with black; apple-mobile-web-app-title names the home-screen icon, which would otherwise take the page's title. {%- endcomment -%}
