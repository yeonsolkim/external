Definition 1.1.9. Let zero be less than or equal to r, which is less than or equal to n. An r-combination of n distinct objects is a complete outcome of a process of selecting r objects without repetition in which the order of selection is irrelevant. The number of r-combinations is denoted by n C r, or n choose r.
