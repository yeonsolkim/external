Theorem 1.1.4, Addition rule. Let A and B be subsets of omega be disjoint and let the number of elements of A, equal m and the number of elements of B, equal n. Then the number of elements of A union B, equals the number of elements of A plus the number of elements of B, which equals m plus n.
