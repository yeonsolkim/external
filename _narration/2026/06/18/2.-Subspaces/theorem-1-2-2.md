Theorem 1.2.2. Let V be a vector space over F, and W a subset of V. Then W is a subspace of V if and only if W has the following three properties.

First, we have the zero vector of V in W. Second, for all u and v in W, we have u plus v in W. Third, for all a in F and v in W, we have a v in W.
