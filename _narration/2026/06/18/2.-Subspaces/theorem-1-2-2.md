Theorem 1.2.2. Let V be a vector space over F, and W a subset of V. Then W is a subspace of V if and only if W has the following three properties: First, we have the zero vector of V belongs to W. Second, for all u and v in W, we have u plus v belongs to W. Third, for all a in F and v in W, we have a v belongs to W.
