Theorem 1.2.2. Let V be a vector space over F, and W a subset of V. Then W is a subspace of V if and only if W has the following three properties.

Property one: the zero vector belongs to W.

Property two: u plus v belongs to W for all u and v in W.

Property three: a v belongs to W for all a in F and v in W.
