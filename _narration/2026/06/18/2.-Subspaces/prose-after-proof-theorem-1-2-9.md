We now ask whether the union of subspaces of V is a subspace of V. It is easily seen that the union contains the zero vector and is closed under scalar multiplication. But the union need not be closed under addition in general. In fact, the union of two subspaces becomes a subspace if and only if one of the subspaces contains the other.
