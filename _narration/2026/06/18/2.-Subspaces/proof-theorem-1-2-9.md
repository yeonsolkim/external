Proof. Let the collection C be a collection of subspaces of V, and let W denote the intersection of the collection C. Since every subspace contains the zero vector, the zero vector of V belongs to W. Let a belong to F and u and v belong to W. Then u and v are elements of each subspace in the collection C. Since each subspace in the collection C is closed under the vector addition and scalar multiplication, u plus v and a v are elements of each subspace in the collection C. Hence u plus v and a v are also elements of W, so that W is a subspace of V by Theorem 1.2.2. This completes the proof.
