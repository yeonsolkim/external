Example 1.2.7. Let A be an n by n matrix with the entries A i j in F. The trace of A is the sum of all the entries of the main diagonal, denoted by t r of A. That is, the trace of A equals A one one plus A two two, and so on, up to A n n. The set of n by n matrices with their traces equal to zero is a subspace of n by n matrices over F.
