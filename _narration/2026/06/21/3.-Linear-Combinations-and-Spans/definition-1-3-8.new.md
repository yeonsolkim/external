Definition 1.3.8. Let V be a vector space over F. Given a finite set I and indexed families, the family u i indexed by I in V and the family a i indexed by I in F, the sum, the sum over i in I of a i u i, is called a linear combination of the family u i indexed by I. The scalars a i are called the coefficients of the linear combination. For a subset S contained in V, a vector v in V is called a linear combination of vectors in S if there exist a finite set I and indexed families, the family s i indexed by I in S and the family a i indexed by I in F, such that v equals the sum over i in I of a i s i.
