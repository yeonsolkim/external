Proof. Extensivity follows from Theorem 1.3.7, part one. Since the span of T is a subspace containing T, the span of T is a subspace also containing S. Since the span of S is the smallest subspace containing S, the span of S is contained in the span of T; span is monotone. Let W equal the span of S. Then W is a subspace of V, which contains W. It therefore follows that the span of W is contained in W by Theorem 1.3.7, part two. Since the span of W contains W by Theorem 1.3.7, part one, we have the span of W equals W; span is idempotent. Therefore span is a closure operator. Proposition 1.3.3 shows that a subset S of V is closed if and only if S is a subspace of V. This completes the proof.
