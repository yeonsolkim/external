Theorem 1.3.18, Exchange property. Let S be a subset of a vector space V, and let u and v belong to V.

First, if u belongs to the set difference of span of S union the set containing v, and span of S, then v belongs to span of S union the set containing u.

Second, under the same hypothesis, span of S union the set containing u equals span of S union the set containing v.
