Corollary 1.3.5. Let M, plus, zero in M, be a commutative monoid, let I and J be finite sets, and let varphi from J to I be a bijection. Then, for every family x i, for i in I, in M, the sum over i in I of x i equals the sum over j in J of x sub varphi of j.
