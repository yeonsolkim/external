Since closure is monotone and idempotent, if S is contained in C, then the closure of S is contained in the closure of C, which equals C. Since closure is extensive, if the closure of S is contained in C, then S is contained in the closure of S, which is contained in C. Conversely, suppose star holds. Since the closure of S is contained in the closure of S, we have S is contained in the closure of S; hence closure is extensive. Since the closure of T is closed, S is contained in the closure of T implies the closure of S is contained in the closure of T; closure is monotone. Since the closure of S is closed, the closure of S is contained in the closure of S implies the closure of the closure of S is contained in the closure of S by star. Extensivity now gives the closure of the closure of S equals the closure of S; closure is idempotent. This completes the proof.
