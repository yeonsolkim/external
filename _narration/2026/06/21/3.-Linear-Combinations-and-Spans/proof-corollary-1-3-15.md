Proof. By Theorem 1.3.13, span of S union T is a subspace. It contains both span of S and span of T by Proposition 1.3.9, part one. Theorem 1.2.12, part two, then gives the inclusion span of S plus span of T is contained in span of S union T. On the other hand, span of S plus span of T is a subspace by Theorem 1.2.12, part one. It contains S union T by the same theorem and extensivity. Corollary 1.3.14 therefore gives the reverse inclusion. This completes the proof.
