In general closure-operator theory, to combine two closed sets is to take the closure of the union of the two sets. In the setting of vector spaces, this construction corresponds to taking the span of two subspaces U and W. Theorems 1.2.12 and 1.3.13 characterize the span of U union W and U plus W by the same universal property: each is the smallest subspace containing both U and W. Therefore, the span of U union W equals U plus W.

Thus, the sum of subspaces is the specific realization of the general construction of combining two closed sets. The next corollary extends this connection from subspaces to arbitrary subsets S and T.
