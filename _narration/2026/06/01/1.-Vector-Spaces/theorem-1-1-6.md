Theorem 1.1.6. Let V be a vector space over a field F.

First, zero times u equals the zero vector for all u in V.

Second, the quantity negative a times u equals negative the quantity a u, which equals a times the quantity negative u, for each a in F and each u in V.

Third, a times the zero vector equals the zero vector for all a in F.
