O — zero matrix (matrix with every entry zero; distinguish from scalar zero)
\mathbf 0 — zero function (function in F^S with value zero everywhere)
0_V — zero vector (additive identity of the vector space V)
F^S — F to the S (set of all functions from S to F)

{F}^{m\times n} — F m by n (set of all m by n matrices over F)
\mathbb Z_{\ge 0} — nonnegative integers (allowed values of the polynomial degree index n)
