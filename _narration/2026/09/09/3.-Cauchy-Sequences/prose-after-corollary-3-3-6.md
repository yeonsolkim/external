The argument in R k reveals a distinction that is more fundamental than compactness itself. The Cauchy condition concerns the asymptotic relation among the terms of a sequence, whereas the assertion that every such sequence has a limit is a property of the ambient metric space. This motivates separating these two roles: a Cauchy sequence has the internal behavior required of a convergent sequence, and a complete space is one in which this behavior is always realized by an actual limit.
