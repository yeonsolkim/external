Proof. One. Let epsilon greater than zero be given, and let x and y belong to the closure of A. Then there exist points x prime and y prime in A such that d of x and x prime is less than epsilon and d of y and y prime is less than epsilon. Hence, we have d of x and y is less than or equal to d of x and x prime plus d of x prime and y prime plus d of y prime and y, which is less than d of x prime and y prime plus two epsilon, which is less than or equal to the diameter of A plus two epsilon. It follows that d of x and y is less than the diameter of A. Therefore the diameter of the closure of A is less than or equal to the diameter of A. Since A is contained in the closure of A, it is clear that the diameter of A is less than or equal to the diameter of the closure of A. Consequently, the diameter of the closure of A equals the diameter of A. Two. By Corollary 2.2.12, the intersection of the K n is nonempty.
