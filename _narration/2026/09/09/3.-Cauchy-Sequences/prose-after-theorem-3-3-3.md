The preceding results focus on describing Cauchyness itself. We now ask whether this internal asymptotic behavior of a sequence is enough to produce convergence. Every convergent sequence is necessarily Cauchy, but the converse requires something from the ambient space. Compactness provides one condition under which this can be guaranteed.
