Lemma 3.3.4. Let M be a metric space. Then the following hold.

First, let A be a subset of M. Then, the diameter of closure of A equals the diameter of A.

Second, let K n be a sequence of nonempty compact sets in M such that K sub n plus one is contained in K n for n equals one, two, three, and so on. If the limit as n tends to infinity of the diameter of K n equals zero, then the intersection of the K n consists of one point.
