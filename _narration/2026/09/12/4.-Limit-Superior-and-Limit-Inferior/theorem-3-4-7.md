Theorem 3.4.7. Let the sequence x n and the sequence y n be sequences in R. If x n is less than or equal to y n eventually, then the following hold.

One. The lim sup of x n is less than or equal to the lim sup of y n.

Two. The lim inf of x n is less than or equal to the lim inf of y n.
