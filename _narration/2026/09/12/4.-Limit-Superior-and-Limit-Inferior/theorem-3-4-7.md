Theorem 3.4.7. Let x n and y n be sequences in R. If x n is less than or equal to y n eventually, then the following hold.

First, the lim sup of x n is less than or equal to the lim sup of y n.

Second, the lim inf of x n is less than or equal to the lim inf of y n.
