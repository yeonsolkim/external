Definition 3.4.2. Let x n be a sequence in R. For each k, define s k equals the supremum of the k-th tail, and t k equals the infimum of the k-th tail.

The limit superior and limit inferior of x n are defined, respectively, by lim sup of x n equals the infimum over k greater than or equal to one of s k, and lim inf of x n equals the supremum over k greater than or equal to one of t k.
