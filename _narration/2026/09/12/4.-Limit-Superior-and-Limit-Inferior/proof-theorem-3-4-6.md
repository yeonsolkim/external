Proof. Let A be the set of all extended subsequential limits of the sequence x n. If x n tends to x then every subsequential limit of the sequence x n is x. Hence A equals the singleton x, so that the maximum of A equals the minimum of A equals x. Suppose, conversely, that the limit superior and limit inferior of the sequence x n are both x. Then for any epsilon greater than zero, eventually x minus epsilon is less than x n, which is less than x plus epsilon, by Theorem 3.4.5. That is, the absolute value of x n minus x is less than epsilon. Hence x n tends to x. This completes the proof.
