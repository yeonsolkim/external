Proof. Suppose x n converges to x and let the sequence x sub n k be a subsequence of the sequence x n. For any epsilon greater than zero, there exists n zero in the positive integers such that n is greater than or equal to n zero implies d of x n and x is less than epsilon. Since the set of n k such that k is greater than or equal to one is infinite, there exists k zero in the positive integers such that k zero is greater than or equal to n zero. Hence, k is greater than or equal to k zero implies d of x sub n k and x is less than epsilon; that is, the sequence x sub n k converges to x. Conversely, suppose every subsequence of the sequence x n converges to x. Put n k equals k. Then the sequence x sub n k equals the sequence x k, which equals the sequence x n. Therefore the sequence x n converges to x. This completes the proof.
