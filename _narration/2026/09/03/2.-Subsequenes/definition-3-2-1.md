Definition 3.2.1. Let M be a metric space. Given a sequence x n, consider a sequence n k in the positive integers, such that n one is less than n two is less than and so on. Then the sequence x sub n k, which maps k to x sub n k, is called a subsequence of x n. If x sub n k converges, its limit is called a subsequential limit of x n.
