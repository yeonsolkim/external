Theorem 1.4.13. Let S be a subset of a vector space V, and let u and v belong to V. Then the following hold.

First, we have u belongs to span of S union the set containing v, set minus span of S, if and only if v belongs to span of S union the set containing u, set minus span of S.

Second, if either of the equivalent conditions in First holds, then span of S union the set containing u equals span of S union the set containing v.
