Definition 3.1.1. Let M be a metric space. A sequence x n of points in M is said to converge in M if there is a point x in M with the following property: For any epsilon greater than zero, there exists n zero in the positive integers such that n greater than or equal to n zero implies d of x n, x is less than epsilon.
