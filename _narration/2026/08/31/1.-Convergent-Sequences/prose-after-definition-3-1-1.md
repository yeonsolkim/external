In this case, we also say that x n converges to x, or that x is the limit of x n, and we write x n goes to x or limit as n goes to infinity of x n equals x. If x n does not converge, it is said to diverge in M.

As can be seen from the definition, convergence depends only on the tail of a sequence: changing finitely many initial terms does not affect either convergence or its limit.
