By Lemma 3.1.7, we obtain d of x n and zero is greater than or equal to d of zero and x minus d of x n and x, which is greater than one half d of zero and x, whenever n greater than or equal to m. Equivalently, the absolute value of x n is greater than one half the absolute value of x.
