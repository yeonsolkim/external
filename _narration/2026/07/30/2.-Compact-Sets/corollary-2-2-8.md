Corollary 2.2.8. Let M be a metric space and let K and F be subsets of M. If K is compact and F is closed, then K intersect F is compact.

Proof. Since K is closed by Theorem 2.2.5 and F is closed, K intersect F is closed by part 2 of Theorem 2.1.15. Since K intersect F is a closed subset of K, Theorem 2.2.7 shows that K intersect F is compact. This completes the proof.
