Definition 2.2.1. Let M be a metric space and let A be contained in M. A collection, the family U i, for i in I, of subsets of M is a cover of A if A is contained in the union over i in I of U i.

A cover of A whose elements are open sets is called an open cover of A. A subcollection of a cover of A that still covers A is called a subcover of A, and a subcover of A whose index set is finite is called a finite subcover of A.
