Proof. Let I n equal the closed interval from a n to b n, and let A be the set of all a n. Then, A is nonempty and bounded above by b one. Let alpha be the supremum for A. If m and n belong to the positive integers then we have a n is less than or equal to a sub m plus n, which is less than b sub m plus n, which is less than or equal to b m, so that b m is an upper bound for A. Therefore a m is less than or equal to alpha, which is less than or equal to b m for every m, so that alpha belongs to the intersection from n equals one to infinity of I n. This completes the proof.
