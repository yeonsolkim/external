Corollary 2.2.18, the Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem. Every bounded infinite subset of R k has a limit point in R k.

Proof. Let A be a bounded infinite subset of R k. Since A is bounded, some k cell I contains A. Since I is compact by Theorem 2.2.16, Theorem 2.2.17 shows that A has a limit point in I. This completes the proof.
