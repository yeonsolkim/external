For y in K, since x is not equal to y, we have d of x and y is greater than zero. Let zero be less than r y, which is less than one half times d of x and y. Since K is compact, there are finitely many points y one through y n in K such that K is contained in the union from i equals one to n of the ball of radius r sub y i about y i, which equals U. Therefore, if r equals the minimum of the set consisting of r sub y one through r sub y n, then the ball of radius r about x does not intersect U. Since K is contained in U, we have K intersected with the ball of radius r about x equals the empty set.
