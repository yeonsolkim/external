Definition 2.2.14. Let k belong to the positive integers, and let a i and b i be reals such that a i is less than b i for i equals one through k. The set of all points x equals x one through x k in R k whose coordinates satisfy a i is less than or equal to x i, which is less than or equal to b i, is called a k cell.
