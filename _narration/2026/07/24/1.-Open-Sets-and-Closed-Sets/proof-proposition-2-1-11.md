Proof. First, if x belongs to the closure of A, then every ball meets A. Hence no balls lie entirely in the complement of A, so that x does not belong to the exterior of A. If x does not belong to the exterior of A, then every ball meets A so that x belongs to the closure of A. Hence the closure of A equals M minus the exterior of A. Second, since the open ball of radius r about x equals the punctured open ball of radius r about x union the set consisting of x, every ball around x meets A if and only if every punctured ball meets A or the set consisting of x meets A. Hence the closure of A equals A union the derived set of A. Third, if x belongs to the interior of A, then the open ball of radius r about x is contained in A for some r greater than zero. Since x belongs to the open ball of radius r about x, x belongs to A, so that the interior of A is contained in A. Part two immediately yields A is contained in the closure of A. Therefore the interior of A is contained in A, which is contained in the closure of A. This completes the proof.
