Proof. Part one. If x belongs to the closure of A, then every ball meets A. Hence no balls lie entirely in A complement, so that x does not belong to the exterior of A. If x does not belong to the exterior of A, then every ball meets A so that x belongs to the closure of A. Hence the closure of A equals M set minus the exterior of A. Part two. Since the open ball of radius r about x equals the union of the punctured open ball of radius r about x and the singleton x, every ball around x meets A if and only if every punctured ball meets A or the singleton x meets A. Hence the closure of A equals the union of A and A prime. Part three. If x belongs to the interior of A, then the open ball of radius r about x is contained in A for some r greater than zero. Since x belongs to the open ball of radius r about x, x belongs to A, so that the interior of A is contained in A. Part two immediately yields A is contained in the closure of A. Therefore the interior of A is contained in A, which is contained in the closure of A. This completes the proof.
