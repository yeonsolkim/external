Theorem 2.1.13. Every open ball is open.

Proof. Let M be a metric space. Define the open ball of radius r about a, for some a in M and r greater than zero. If x is in the open ball of radius r about a, then d of x, a is less than r. We choose s greater than zero so that s is less than r minus d of x, a. Since, for any y in M, d of y, x less than s implies d of y, a is less than or equal to d of y, x plus d of x, a, which is less than s plus d of x, a, which is less than r, we have the open ball of radius s about x is contained in the open ball of radius r about a. Hence the ball, the open ball of radius r about a, is open. This completes the proof.
