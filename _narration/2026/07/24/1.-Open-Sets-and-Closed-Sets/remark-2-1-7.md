Remark 2.1.7. Since every ball B r of x contains x and hence is nonempty, no x in M can be an interior point, boundary point, limit point, or closure point of the empty set. On the other hand, every x in M is an exterior point of the empty set. In the case of M, every x in M is an interior point and closure point of M, while no x in M can be a boundary point or an exterior point of M.
