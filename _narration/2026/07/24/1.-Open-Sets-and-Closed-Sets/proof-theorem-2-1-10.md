Proof. Since alpha is the supremum for A, for every r greater than zero, there exists x in A such that alpha minus r is less than x, which is less than or equal to alpha. Hence the open ball of radius r about alpha has a point of A for every r greater than zero. Therefore alpha is a closure point of A. This completes the proof.
