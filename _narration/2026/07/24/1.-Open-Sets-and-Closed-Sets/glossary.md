\mathbb Z^{+} — positive integers (the index \(k\) ranges over positive integers)

B_r(a) — the open ball of radius r about a (also \(B_r(x)\), \(B_s(x)\), etc.)

\overline B_r(a) — the closed ball of radius r about a (distinguish from \(\overline A\), the closure of a set)

B_r^{\ast}(a) — the punctured open ball of radius r about a (also \(B_r^{\ast}(x)\))

\operatorname{int} A — the interior of A (in the ambient metric space unless a subscript says otherwise)

\operatorname{ext} A — the exterior of A (in the ambient metric space)

\partial A — the boundary of A (in the ambient metric space)

\overline A — the closure of A (distinguish from \(\overline B_r(a)\), the closed ball)

A' — the derived set of A (the set of limit points of \(A\))

A^c — the complement of A (complement in the ambient metric space)

\dot{\cup} — disjoint union (used for the partition into interior, boundary, and exterior)

\lbrace U_i\rbrace_{i\in I} — the family U i (a family of open sets indexed by \(I\))

\lbrace F_i\rbrace_{i\in I} — the family F i (a family of closed sets indexed by \(I\))

\bigcap_{\varnothing}U_i — the empty intersection of the U i (by convention equals \(M\))

\lowparen{\cdots}^{c} — the complement of the parenthesized expression (\(\lowparen{}\) is only lowered parentheses; read normally)

B^N_r(x) — the ball in N (open ball in the subspace \(N\))

B^M_r(x) — the ball in M (open ball in the ambient space \(M\))

\tag{$\ast$} — star (the displayed identity \(B^N_{r_x}(x)=B^M_{r_x}(x)\cap N\); later referred to as “star”)

\overline A^{\raise{-0.5em}{N}} — the closure of A in N (the raised \(N\) marks the ambient space)

\overline A^{\raise{-0.5em}{M}} — the closure of A in M (the raised \(M\) marks the ambient space)

\operatorname{int}_N A — the interior of A in N (subspace interior)

\operatorname{int}_M A — the interior of A in M (ambient interior)
