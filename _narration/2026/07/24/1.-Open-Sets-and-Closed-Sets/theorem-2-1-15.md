Theorem 2.1.15. Let M be a metric space.

First, if the family U i, for i in I, is a collection of open sets, then the union over i in I of U i is open.

Second, if the family F i, for i in I, is a collection of closed sets, then the intersection over i in I of F i is closed.

Third, if the family U i, for i in I, is a finite collection of open sets, then the intersection over i in I of U i is open.

Fourth, if the family F i, for i in I, is a finite collection of closed sets, then the union over i in I of F i is closed.
