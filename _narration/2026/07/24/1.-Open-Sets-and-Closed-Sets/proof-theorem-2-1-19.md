Proof. If x belongs to the closure of A in N, then every open ball in N of radius r about x meets A. Thus the open ball in M of radius r about x meets A. Hence x belongs to the closure of A in M and thus x belongs to the intersection of the closure of A in M with N. If x belongs to the intersection of the closure of A in M with N, then every open ball in M of radius r about x meets A. Since A is contained in N, the intersection point must be in N. Thus, even if we take the intersection of the open ball in M of radius r about x with N, it still meets A; that is, the open ball in N of radius r about x meets A. Therefore x belongs to the closure of A in N. This completes the proof.
