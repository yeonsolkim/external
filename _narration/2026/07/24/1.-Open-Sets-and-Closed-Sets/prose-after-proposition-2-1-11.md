So far, our notions have described the local relations of an individual point to a set. We now turn these pointwise relations into global properties of a subset of a metric space as a whole. Recall that, for a subset A of a metric space M, the interior of A is contained in A, which is contained in the closure of A.

The set A is open in M when the first inclusion is an equality, and closed in M when the second inclusion is an equality.
