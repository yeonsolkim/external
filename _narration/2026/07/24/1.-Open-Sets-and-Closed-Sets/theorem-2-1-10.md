Theorem 2.1.10. Let A be a nonempty subset of R which is bounded above, and let alpha equal the supremum of A. Then alpha belongs to the closure of A.

Proof. Since alpha is the supremum for A, there exists x in A such that alpha minus r is less than x, which is less than or equal to alpha, for every r greater than zero. Hence the open ball of radius r about alpha has a point of A for every r greater than zero. Therefore alpha is a closure point of A. This completes the proof.
