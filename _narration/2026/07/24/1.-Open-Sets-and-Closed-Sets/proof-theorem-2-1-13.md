Proof. Let M be a metric space. Define the open ball of radius r about a for some a in M and r greater than zero. If x belongs to the open ball of radius r about a, then d of x and a is less than r. We choose s greater than zero so that s is less than r minus d of x and a. Since, for any y in M, d of y and x is less than s implies d of y and a is less than or equal to d of y and x plus d of x and a, which is less than s plus d of x and a, which is less than r, we have the open ball of radius s about x is contained in the open ball of radius r about a. Hence the ball B r of a is open. This completes the proof.
