Definition 2.1.3. Let M be a metric space, let a belong to M, and let r be greater than zero. The open ball of radius r centered at a is the set B r of a defined by the open ball of radius r about a equals the set of x in M such that d of x and a is less than r.

The closed ball of radius r centered at a is the set closed ball of radius r about a defined by the closed ball of radius r about a equals the set of x in M such that d of x and a is less than or equal to r.

The punctured open ball of radius r centered at a is the set punctured open ball of radius r about a defined by the punctured open ball of radius r about a equals the set of x in M such that zero is less than d of x and a, which is less than r.
