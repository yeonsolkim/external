Proposition 2.1.11. Let M be a metric space and let A be a subset of M. Then the following hold.

One. The closure of A equals M minus the exterior of A.

Two. The closure of A equals A union the derived set of A.

Three. The interior of A is contained in A, which is contained in the closure of A.
