Proposition 2.1.11. Let M be a metric space and let A be a subset of M. Then the following hold.

First, the closure of A equals M set minus the exterior of A.

Second, the closure of A equals A union A prime.

Third, the interior of A is contained in A, which is contained in the closure of A.
