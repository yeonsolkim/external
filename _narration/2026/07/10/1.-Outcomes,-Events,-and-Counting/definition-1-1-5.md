Definition 1.1.5. A process is an experiment that is completed in successive stages, whose outcome is called the complete outcome. We let A be the event of the first stage and B the event of the second stage, with the number of elements of A equals m and the number of elements of B equals n. Then the event “A occurs and then B occurs” is denoted by A cross B.
