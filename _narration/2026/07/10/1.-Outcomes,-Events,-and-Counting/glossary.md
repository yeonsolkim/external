\Omega — omega (the sample space)
\omega_{\text{actual}} — the actual outcome (the outcome that occurred)
|A| — the number of elements of A (cardinality; read as number of ways event A can occur when A is an event)
A^c — A complement (the event that A does not occur)
\varnothing — the empty event (empty set, when used as an event)
A\times B — A then B (two-stage event: A occurs and then B occurs; not just a Cartesian product here)
n! — n factorial (product n times n minus one down to one)
{}_nP_r — n P r (number of r-permutations of n objects)
{}_nC_r — n C r (number of r-combinations of n objects)
\binom nr — n choose r (same number as n C r)
x_i — x i (the occurrence of x from the i-th factor; subscript records provenance)
y_i — y i (the occurrence of y from the i-th factor; subscript records provenance)
x_i\prod_{j\ne i}y_j — x i times the product over j not equal to i of y j (a raw term provenance)
\prod_{j\ne i}y_j — the product over j not equal to i of y j (product of all y factors except the i-th one)
