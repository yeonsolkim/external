Each r-combination gives rise to r factorial different r-permutations by arranging its selected objects in every possible order. Therefore, n P r equals n C r times r factorial, and consequently n C r equals n P r over r factorial, which equals n factorial, over the product of r factorial and the factorial of n minus r.

Since choosing r objects to include is equivalent to choosing the remaining n minus r objects to exclude, we also have n C r equals n C the quantity n minus r.
