9. Corollaries.

Setting x equals y equals one in the binomial theorem gives the sum from r equals zero to n of n choose r equals two to the n.

Since every subset of an n-element set has a unique cardinality r, the sum on the left counts all subsets of the set. Thus an n-element set has two to the n subsets.
