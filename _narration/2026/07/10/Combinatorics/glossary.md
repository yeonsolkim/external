\Omega — omega (the sample space)
|A| — size of A (number of outcomes in event A; not absolute value here)
A\times B — A then B (two-stage event, not just ordinary multiplication)
A_1\times\cdots\times A_k — A one through A k (multi-stage event)
n! — n factorial
{}_nP_r — n P r (number of r-permutations of n distinct objects)
{}_nC_r — n choose r (number of r-combinations of n distinct objects)
\binom nr — n choose r (same as {}_nC_r; later also the binomial coefficient)
\underbrace{(x+y)(x+y)\cdots(x+y)}_{n\text{ factors}} — n factors (brace label)
x_i,y_i — x i, y i (labelled occurrences recording provenance in the binomial theorem proof)
