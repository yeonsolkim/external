Types of buy-side traders: Investors and borrowers. If you have surplus money today but will need it in the future, you become an investor. For example, if you have ten thousand dollars today and want to use it in ten years, you may buy stocks or bonds to transfer wealth from the present to the future.

Present wealth to investment to future wealth.

Conversely, if you need money today but expect to earn income in the future, you become a borrower. Typical examples include a student taking out a student loan or a young couple using a mortgage to buy a home.

Future income to borrowing to cash available today.

Therefore, investors move money from the present to the future, while borrowers move money from the future to the present.
