Proof. Suppose, for contradiction, that there exists a punctured open ball of a which contains only a finite number of points of A. Since a is a limit point of A, we may let p one through p n be those points, which are distinct from a. We now define r equals the minimum, for i from one to n, of d of a and p i, and it is clear that r is greater than zero. Then, the punctured open ball of radius r about a contains no point of A. Thus a is not a limit point of A, which contradicts our premise. This completes the proof.
