Proof. Let M be a metric space. Define the open ball of radius r about a for some a in M and r greater than zero. If x belongs to the open ball of radius r about a, then d of x and a, is less than r. We choose s greater than zero so that s is less than r minus d of x and a. Since, for any y in M, d of y and x, less than s, implies d of y and a, is less than or equal to d of y and x plus d of x and a, which is less than s plus d of x and a, which is less than r, we have, the open ball of radius s about x, is contained in the open ball of radius r about a. Hence the ball the open ball of radius r about a, is open. This completes the proof.
