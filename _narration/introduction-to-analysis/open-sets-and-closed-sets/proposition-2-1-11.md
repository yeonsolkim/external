Proposition 2.1.11. Let M be a metric space and let A be contained in M. Then the following hold: first, the closure of A equals M set minus the exterior of A. Second, the closure of A equals A union the derived set of A. Third, the interior of A is contained in A, which is contained in the closure of A.
