Proof. If x belongs to the closure of A in N, then every open ball in N, B r of x, of x, meets A. Thus the open ball in M, B r of x, meets A. Hence x belongs to the closure of A in M and thus x belongs to the closure of A in M, intersect N. If x belongs to the closure of A in M, intersect N, then every open ball in M, B r of x, of x, meets A. Since A is contained in N, the intersection point must be in N. Thus, even if we take the intersection of the open ball in M, B r of x, with N, it still meets A; that is, the open ball in N, B r of x, meets A. Therefore x belongs to the closure of A in N. This completes the proof.
