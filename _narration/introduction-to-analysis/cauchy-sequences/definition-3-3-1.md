Definition 3.3.1. Let M be a metric space and let x n be a sequence in M. The sequence x n is called a Cauchy sequence if for any epsilon greater than zero, there exists n naught in the positive integers such that we have m and n greater than or equal to n naught implies d of x m and x n is less than epsilon.
