Proof. Let epsilon greater than zero be given. Then eventually y n is less than epsilon. Since x n is less than or equal to y n, we have zero is less than or equal to x n, which is less than epsilon, eventually. Since epsilon greater than zero was arbitrary, x n tends to zero. This completes the proof.
