Theorem 3.4.7. Let the sequence x n and the sequence y n be sequences in R. If x n is less than or equal to y n eventually, then the following hold: One: the limit superior of x n is less than or equal to the limit superior of y n. Two: the limit inferior of x n is less than or equal to the limit inferior of y n.
