Definition 3.4.2. Let x n be a sequence in R. For each k, define, s k equals the supremum of the set of x n such that n is greater than or equal to k, and t k equals the infimum of the set of x n such that n is greater than or equal to k.

The limit superior and limit inferior of x n are defined, respectively, by the limit superior of x n, equals the infimum, over k greater than or equal to one, of s k, and the limit inferior of x n, equals the supremum, over k greater than or equal to one, of t k.
