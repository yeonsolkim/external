Proof. If v belongs to the span of S, then there exists a finitely supported function a from S to F such that v equals the sum over s in the support of a of a of s times s, by Proposition 1.4.14. Put S naught equal to the support of a and define the restriction a restricted to S naught of a. Then S naught is contained in S, and v belongs to the span of S naught by Proposition 1.4.14. The converse is trivial: since S naught is contained in S, extensivity gives v belongs to the span of S naught, which is contained in the span of S. This completes the proof.
