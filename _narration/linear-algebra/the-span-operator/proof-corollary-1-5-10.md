Proof. By Theorems 1.5.7 and 1.5.8, the span of S union T is a subspace. It contains both the span of S and the span of T by Proposition 1.5.3, part one. Theorem 1.3.12, part two, then gives the inclusion the span of S plus the span of T is contained in the span of S union T. On the other hand, the span of S plus the span of T is a subspace by Theorem 1.3.12, part one. It contains S union T by the same theorem and extensivity. Corollary 1.5.9 therefore gives the reverse inclusion. This completes the proof.
