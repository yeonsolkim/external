Theorem 1.5.13. Let S be a subset of a vector space V, and let u and v belong to V. Then the following hold: Part one. We have u belongs to the span of S union the singleton containing v, set minus the span of S, if and only if v belongs to the span of S union the singleton containing u, set minus the span of S. Part two. If either of the equivalent conditions in part one holds, then the span of S union the singleton containing u equals the span of S union the singleton containing v.
