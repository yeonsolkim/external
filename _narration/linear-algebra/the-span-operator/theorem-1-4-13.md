Theorem 1.4.13. Let S be a subset of a vector space V, and let u and v be in V. Then the following hold: First, we have u belongs to the span of the union of S and the singleton set containing v, minus the span of S, if and only if v belongs to the span of the union of S and the singleton set containing u, minus the span of S. Second, if either of the equivalent conditions in one holds, then the span of the union of S and the singleton set containing u equals the span of the union of S and the singleton set containing v.
