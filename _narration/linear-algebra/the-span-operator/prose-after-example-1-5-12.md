Fix a subset S of V. We say that the u-adding effect with respect to v occurs if adjoining u makes S newly generate v. Then, saying that no such effect occurs means that either v was already generated by S or it remains ungenerated after adjoining u. The span operator has two notable properties in connection with this adding effects. First, it exhibits a certain symmetry: The u-adding effect with respect to v occurs if and only if the v-adding effect with respect to u occurs. Equivalently, the absence of such an effect is also reciprocal. Second, whenever an adding effect occurs, the two vectors can be exchanged as additional generators without changing the resulting span. In particular, this property is called the exchange property of the span operator.
