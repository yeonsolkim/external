Definition 1.2.16. Let W be a subspace of a vector space V over a field F. The set of all cosets of W in V is denoted by V modulo W equals the set of all v plus W such that v belongs to V, and is read V modulo W. For u and v in V and a in F, define addition and scalar multiplication on V modulo W by the coset u plus W, plus the coset v plus W, equals the quantity u plus v, plus W, and a times the coset v plus W equals a v plus W.
