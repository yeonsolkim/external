Proof. Let the family W i, for i in I, be any collection of subspaces of V, and let W denote the intersection of the family W i, for i in I. Since every subspace contains the zero vector, the zero vector of V belongs to W. Let a belong to F and u and v belong to W. Then u and v are elements of every subspace W i. Since each subspace W i is closed under addition and scalar multiplication, u plus v and a v are elements of every subspace W i. Hence u plus v and a v are also elements of W, so W is a subspace of V by Theorem 1.3.2. This completes the proof.
