Proof. Suppose W one union W two is a subspace of V, and suppose x belongs to W one set minus W two, and y belongs to W two set minus W one. Then x plus y belongs to W one union W two and, without loss of generality, x plus y belongs to W one. Since W one is a subspace of V, we have y equals the quantity x plus y, plus negative x, belongs to W one. This contradicts y belongs to W two set minus W one. Therefore if W one union W two is a subspace of V then W one is contained in W two or W two is contained in W one. Since W one and W two are subspaces of V, the converse automatically holds. This completes the proof.
