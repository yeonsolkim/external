Proof. Suppose that W is a subspace of V. By Definition 1.3.1, 2 and 3 hold. Since there exists the zero vector of W in W such that v plus the zero vector of W equals v for all v in W and the zero vector of W equals the zero vector of V, 1 holds. Conversely, suppose that 1, 2, and 3 hold. Then, W is closed under scalar multiplication and vector addition. Since minus v equals minus one times v, which belongs to W for all v in W, each element of W has its additive inverse in W. Therefore W is a subspace of V. This completes the proof.
