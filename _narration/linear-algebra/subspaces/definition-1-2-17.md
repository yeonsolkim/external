Definition 1.2.17. Let W be a subspace of a vector space V over a field F. The set of all cosets of W in V is denoted by V modulo W equals the set of all v plus W such that v belongs to V, and is read “V modulo W”. For u and v in V and a in F, define addition and scalar multiplication on V modulo W by u plus W, plus v plus W, equals u plus v, plus W, and a times v plus W, equals a v plus W.
