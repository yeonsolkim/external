Proof. Since S and T are contained in V, we have S union T is contained in V and S intersect T is contained in V. Let A belong to the power set of V. If A contains S and A contains T, then A contains S union T. Hence S union T is the supremum for S and T. On the other hand, if A is contained in S and A is contained in T, then A is contained in S intersect T. Hence S intersect T is the infimum for S and T. By Proposition 1.3.7, the power set of V with union and the power set of V with intersection are an upper semilattice and lower semilattice, respectively. Furthermore, since S intersect T is contained in S and S is contained in S union T, we have, the intersection of S and the union of S and T, equals S, and the union of S and the intersection of S and T, equals S, for all S and T in the power set of V. By Definition 1.3.9, the power set of V with union and intersection is a lattice. This completes the proof.
