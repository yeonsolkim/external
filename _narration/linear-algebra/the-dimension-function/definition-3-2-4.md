Definition 3.2.4. Let P, less than or equal in P, and Q, less than or equal in Q, be posets, and let F from P to Q, and G from Q to P, be maps. The pair F and G is called a Galois connection between these posets if, for every p in P and q in Q, F of p, is less than or equal in Q to q, if and only if p is less than or equal in P to G of q.

In this case, F is called the left adjoint of G, and G is called the right adjoint of F. We write, F is left adjoint to G.
