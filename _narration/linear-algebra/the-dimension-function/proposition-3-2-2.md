Proposition 3.2.2. Let V be a vector space. Then, the partially ordered set the subspaces of V is a lattice, with join and meet given by U join W equals U plus W, and U meet W equals U intersect W, for all U and W in the subspaces of V. The least and greatest elements of the subspaces of V are the zero subspace and V, respectively.
