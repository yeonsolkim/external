Remark 3.2.8. In category theory, every poset P is a category whose objects are the elements of P, with a unique morphism from p to p prime whenever p is less than or equal to p prime, and no morphism otherwise. Moreover, monotone maps between two posets are functors, and a Galois connection is precisely an adjunction between the corresponding categories. Consequently, the power set of V and the subspaces of V, regarded as categories, are related by the adjoint functors sigma is left adjoint to iota.
