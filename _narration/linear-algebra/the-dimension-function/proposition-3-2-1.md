Proposition 3.2.1. Let V be a vector space. Then, the partially ordered set the power set of V is a lattice, with join and meet given by, S join T equals S union T, and S meet T equals S intersect T, for all S and T in the power set of V. Its least and greatest elements are the empty set and V, respectively.
