Theorem 3.2.5. Suppose that two lattices L and M have a Galois connection, and F and G are the left adjoint and the right adjoint, respectively. Then the following hold: First, F and G are monotone. Second, F and G preserve join and meet, respectively. Third, G composed with F is a closure operator.
