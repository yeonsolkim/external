Remark 3.2.13. The difference between the two sides of the submodular inequality can be expressed more precisely as, r of S plus r of T, minus r of the join of S and T, minus r of the meet of S and T, equals rho of the meet of sigma of S and sigma of T, minus rho of sigma of the meet of S and T. Since sigma of the join of S and T is less than or equal to the join of sigma of S and sigma of T, the submodular inequality becomes equality if and only if the span of the intersection of S and T, equals the intersection of the span of S and the span of T.
