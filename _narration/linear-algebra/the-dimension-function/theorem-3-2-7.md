Theorem 3.2.7. Let sigma be the span map, and let iota from the subspaces of V into the power set of V be the inclusion map. Then the pair sigma and iota is a Galois connection between the power set of V and the subspaces of V. Hence the composite, iota composed with sigma, from the power set of V to the power set of V, is a closure operator on the power set of V.
