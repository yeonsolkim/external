Galois connections between the two lattices. The subset lattice, the power set of V, and the subspace lattice, the subspaces of V, exhibit fundamentally different order-theoretic structures. Nevertheless, there is a natural relationship between them, Galois connections. We first introduce this concept for arbitrary posets and then apply it to the span and inclusion maps. Along the way, the span operator is reinterpreted as a map naturally arising from the relationship between the two lattices.
