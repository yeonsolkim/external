The subset lattice and subspace lattice. We first demonstrate that the power set of V of a vector space V and the collection the subspaces of V of all subspaces of V are lattices. In particular, the power set of V is a distributive lattice and the subspaces of V is a modular one. This order-theoretic perspective will help us appreciate the concept of dimension.
