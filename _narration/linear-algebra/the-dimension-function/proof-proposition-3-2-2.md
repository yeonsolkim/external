Proof. Since U plus W is a subspace of V and is the supremum for U and W by Theorem 2.2.12, the structure consisting of the subspaces of V with plus, is an upper semilattice. On the other hand, by Theorem 2.2.9, U intersect W is a subspace of W and is the infimum for U and W. Hence the structure consisting of the subspaces of V with intersection, is a lower semilattice. Furthermore, since U is contained in U plus W and U intersect W is contained in U, we have, U intersect the quantity U plus W, equals U, and U plus the quantity U intersect W, equals U, for all U and W in the subspaces of V. By Definition 1.3.9, the structure consisting of the subspaces of V with plus and intersection, is a lattice. This completes the proof.
