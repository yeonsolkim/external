Proof. Since the subspaces of V is a modular lattice and rho is the rank function, by Theorem 1.3.20, rho is modular. Hence we have, rho of U join W, minus rho of U, equals rho of W, minus rho of U meet W, for all U and W in the subspaces of V. By Proposition 3.2.2 and the definition of rho, this is equivalent to, dimension of U plus W, minus dimension of U, equals dimension of W, minus dimension of U intersect W. Rearranging gives the desired identity. This completes the proof.
