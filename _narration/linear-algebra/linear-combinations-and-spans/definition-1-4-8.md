Definition 1.4.8. Let V be a vector space over F. Given a finite set I and indexed families, the family u i, for i in I, in V, and the family a i, for i in I, in F, the sum over i in I of a i times u i is called a linear combination of the family u i, for i in I. The scalars a i are called the coefficients of the linear combination. For a subset S contained in V, a vector v in V is called a linear combination of vectors in S if there exist a finite set I and indexed families, the family s i, for i in I, in S, and the family a i, for i in I, in F, such that v equals the sum over i in I of a i times s i.
