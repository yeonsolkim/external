Further properties of the span operator. The span operator has further properties that distinguish it from an arbitrary closure operator. They will be especially important in the study of linear dependence, bases, and dimension developed in the following sections. One of them is finite character: although a generating set may be infinite, membership in its span is always witnessed by finitely many generators.
