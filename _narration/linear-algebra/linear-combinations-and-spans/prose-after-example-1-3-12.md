By definition, a vector v belongs to span of S if and only if it can be expressed as a linear combination of vectors in S. However, the same vector in S may occur at several indices, and the corresponding coefficients can be combined without changing the sum. This suggests an essentially concise characterization of span membership: the existence of a representation of v in terms of S. We formalize this idea through finitely supported functions.
