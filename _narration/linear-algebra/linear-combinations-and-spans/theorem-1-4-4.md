Theorem 1.4.4. Let M, plus, zero in M, be a commutative monoid, let I and J be finite sets, and let f from I to J be a function. Then, for every family x i indexed by I in M, the sum over i in I of x i equals the sum over j in J of the sum over i in f inverse of j of x i.
