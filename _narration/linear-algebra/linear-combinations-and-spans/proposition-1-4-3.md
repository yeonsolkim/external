Proposition 1.4.3. Let M, plus, zero in M be a commutative monoid, and let I be a finite set. If I equals the disjoint union of A and B, then for every indexed family x i, for i in I, in M, the sum over i in I of x i equals the sum over i in A of x i plus the sum over i in B of x i.
