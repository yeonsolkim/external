Theorem 1.3.18. Let S be a subset of a vector space V, and let u and v be in V. Then the following hold: First, we have u belongs to the set difference of span of S union the set containing v and span of S, if and only if v belongs to the set difference of span of S union the set containing u and span of S. Second, if either of the equivalent conditions in first holds, then span of S union the set containing u equals span of S union the set containing v.
