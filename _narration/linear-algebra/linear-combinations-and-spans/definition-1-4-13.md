Definition 1.4.13. Let S be a subset of a vector space V over a field F, and let v belong to V. A representation of v in terms of S is a function a from S to F with finite support such that v equals the sum over s in the support of a of a of s times s, and the support of a equals the set of s in S such that a of s is not equal to zero.
