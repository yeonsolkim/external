Proposition 1.4.7. Let V be a vector space over a field F, and let I be a finite set. Then the following hold: First, for any families u i indexed by I and v i indexed by I in V, the sum over i in I of the quantity u i plus v i equals the sum over i in I of u i, plus the sum over i in I of v i. Second, for any family v i indexed by I in V and any scalar a in F, the sum over i in I of a times v i equals a times the sum over i in I of v i. Third, for any family a i indexed by I in F and any vector v in V, the sum over i in I of a i times v equals the quantity given by the sum over i in I of a i, times v.
