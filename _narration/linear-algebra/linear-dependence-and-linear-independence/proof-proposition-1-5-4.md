Proof. Suppose that S is linearly dependent. By Proposition 1.5.3, there exists v in S such that v belongs to the span of S without v. Since S without v is contained in T without v, monotonicity gives v belongs to the span of S without v, which is contained in the span of T without v.

Hence T is linearly dependent by Proposition 1.5.3. Taking the contrapositive of part one yields part two. This completes the proof.
