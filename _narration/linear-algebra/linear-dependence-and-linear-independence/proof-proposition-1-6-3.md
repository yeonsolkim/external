Proof. Since S without v is contained in S, monotonicity gives the span of S without v is contained in the span of S.

By Theorem 1.5.4, the reverse inclusion holds if and only if S is contained in the span of S without v.

Since S without v is already contained in its span, this is equivalent to v belonging to the span of S without v. This completes the proof.
