Proposition 1.5.8. Let V be a vector space over a field F, and let S be a subset of V.

First, the set S is linearly dependent if the zero vector in V belongs to S. Second, the empty set is linearly independent. Third, the set S is linearly independent if it consists of a single nonzero vector.
