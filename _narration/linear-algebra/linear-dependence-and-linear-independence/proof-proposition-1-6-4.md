Proof. Suppose that S is linearly dependent. By Proposition 1.6.3, there exists v in S such that v belongs to span of S setminus v. Since S setminus v is contained in T setminus v, monotonicity gives v belongs to span of S setminus v, which is contained in span of T setminus v.

Hence T is linearly dependent by Proposition 1.6.3. Taking the contrapositive of part one yields part two. This completes the proof.
