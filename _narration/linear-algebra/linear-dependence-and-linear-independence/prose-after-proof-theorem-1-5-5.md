In the language of linear algebra, the redundancy of a subset of a vector space can be expressed without referring to one particular element. For example, the redundancy of the set S in Example 1.5.1 can be expressed as follows: the sum over s in S of a sub s times s equals the zero vector.

That is, a redundant generator gives rise to a nontrivial representation of the zero vector in terms of S. This gives an algebraic formulation of linear dependence.
