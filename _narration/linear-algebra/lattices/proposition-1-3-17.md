Proposition 1.3.17. A lattice L is graded if and only if all maximal chains in any interval in L have the same length. In this case, the rank function is unique and satisfies, rho of x equals the length of the interval from zero to x, and the length of the interval from x to y equals rho of y minus rho of x, for every x and y in L with x is less than or equal to y.
