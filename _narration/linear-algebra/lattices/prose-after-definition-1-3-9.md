In a lattice L, the join and meet operations each determine an order relation. The first identity in L three ensures that if y is an upper bound for x with respect to the join order, then x is a lower bound for y with respect to the meet order for all x and y in L. The second identity gives the converse implication. Thus the two induced order relations coincide.
