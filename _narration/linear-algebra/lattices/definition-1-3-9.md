Definition 1.3.9. A lattice is an ordered triple L with join and meet where L is a set and join and meet are binary operations on L satisfying the following: First, L with join is an upper semilattice. Second, L with meet is a lower semilattice. Third, for all x and y in L, x meet the quantity x join y, equals x, and x join the quantity x meet y, equals x.
