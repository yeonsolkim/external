Definition 1.3.5. A nonempty poset P is said to have finite length if there exists a nonnegative integer n such that every chain in P is finite and has length at most n. The least such n is called the length of P, denoted by ell of P. In particular, the length of a finite-length poset P is the maximum of the lengths of all chains in P.
