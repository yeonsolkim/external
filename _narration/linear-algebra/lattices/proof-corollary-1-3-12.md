Proof. Fix x in L and define, f sub x from L to L, by f sub x of t equals x join t.

Suppose y and z are in L and y is less than or equal to z. Since x join z is greater than or equal to x and x join z is greater than or equal to z, which is greater than or equal to y, x join z is an upper bound for x and y. By the definition of supremum, x join y is less than or equal to x join z, so that f sub x of y is less than or equal to f sub x of z. Thus f sub x is monotone. Theorem 1.3.11 now gives the first inequality. The second inequality follows from duality. This completes the proof.
