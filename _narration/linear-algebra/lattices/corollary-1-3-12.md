Corollary 1.3.12. Let L be a lattice. For all x, y, z in L, x join the quantity y meet z, is less than or equal to, the quantity x join y, meet the quantity x join z, and x meet the quantity y join z, is greater than or equal to, the quantity x meet y, join the quantity x meet z.
