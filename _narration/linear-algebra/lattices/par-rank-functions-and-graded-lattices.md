Rank functions and graded lattices. The preceding results describe modularity and semimodularity through relations among joins, meets, and covering pairs. To express these structural conditions numerically, we seek a function that assigns a level to each element and increases by one across every covering relation. We then examine inequalities involving the ranks of joins and meets, which lead to numerical characterizations of semimodularity and modularity. Throughout the remainder of this section, we only consider finite-length lattices with their least element.
