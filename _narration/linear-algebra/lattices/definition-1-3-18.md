Definition 1.3.18. Let L be a lattice and let f from L to R be a function. We say that f is submodular if, for all x and y in L, f of the quantity x join y, minus f of y, is less than or equal to f of x, minus f of the quantity x meet y.

Dually, f is called supermodular if, for all x and y in L, f of the quantity x join y, minus f of y, is greater than or equal to f of x, minus f of the quantity x meet y.

We say that f is modular if it is both submodular and supermodular.
