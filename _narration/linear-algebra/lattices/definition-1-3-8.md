Definition 1.3.8. A semilattice S with join is called an upper semilattice if x join y is interpreted as the supremum for the set consisting of x and y for all x and y in S. In this case, the operation join is called join. Replacing “supremum” with “infimum”, we obtain the dual concept of a lower semilattice. To distinguish from the previous situation, we shall denote a lower semilattice by S with meet and call meet meet.
