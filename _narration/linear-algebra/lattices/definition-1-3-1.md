Definition 1.3.1. Let P be a poset. For x and y in P, we say that y covers x, written x is covered by y, if x is less than y and there is no z in P such that x is less than z, which is less than y. Equivalently, we have, x is covered by y if and only if the interval from x to y equals the set consisting of x and y. In this case, x is called the immediate predecessor of y, and y is called the immediate successor of x.
