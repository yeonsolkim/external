Theorem 1.2.6. Let V be a vector space over a field F.

First, for all u in V, we have zero times u equals the zero vector. Second, for each a in F and each u in V, we have negative a times u equals the negative of a u, which equals a times negative u. Third, for all a in F, we have a times the zero vector equals the zero vector.
