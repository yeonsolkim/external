Definition 1.2.1. Let F be a field. Then, a vector space over F consists of an abelian group V under addition, together with a scalar multiplication from F times V to V such that, for all a and b in F and u and v in V, first, one times v equals v. Second, the quantity a b times v equals a times the quantity b v. Third, a times the quantity u plus v equals a u plus a v. And fourth, the quantity a plus b times v equals a v plus b v.

The elements of a vector space are called vectors.
