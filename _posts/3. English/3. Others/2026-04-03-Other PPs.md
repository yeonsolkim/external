### by-PP
1. reason: *it was marked by a good or legitimate reason.*
2. unit: *they are paid by the week.*
3. determining factor: *my scheme would stand or fall by that principle.*


### with-PP
1. the complement of an adjective: *I fell so ill with hay fever.*
2. manner: *he solved the problem with great difficulty.*
3. content: *it worked out with her help.*


### as-PP
1. means: *they ascertained exactly as a result of research or calculation.*
2. (circumstantial/temporal) background: *we join Wilson in 1911 as he wonders whether he can re-create the scene in the laboratory.*
3. content: *the conference was acclaimed as a considerable success.*


### about-PP
1. domain: *there is much to learn about current problems.*


### to-PP
1. purpose: *he uses his power to bad effect.*


### for-PP
1. local purpose of a speaker.
2. the subject's intended treatment of the object: *they submitted a proposal or theory for consideration.*
