### can
1. ability: *he can lift 500N.*
2. permission: *you can go home now.*
3. circumstantial possibility: *even expert drivers can make mistakes \| water can boil \| the absence of legs cannot disguise their affinities with mammals \| I can't believe that we are faced with that sort of choice.*
4. conjecture: *she can't be tired now.*


### may
1. permission.
2. conjecture.
3. circumstantial possibility.


### must, have 
1. duty: *you must do military service.*
2. necessity: *I have to call my mom.*
3. strong conjecture: *He must be hungry.*

### should, ought
1. expectation based on common sense: *it should be obvious that humans walk on two feet.*
2. duty.


### will
1. volition: *I will lose weight.*
2. cooperativeness: *the door won't open.*
3. disposition: *after sad things, happy things will happen.*
4. definite conjecture: *he will be a doctor \| they had imagined that whatever or whoever it was that controlled their surroundings would do so.*


### would
1. a polite opinion or suggestion: *I would be happy to help \| would you like some help?*
2. past habit: *I would sometimes travel alone.*
