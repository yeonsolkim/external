### functioning in an NP
1. attribute: *a man of courage.*
2. carrier: *the color of the sky.*
3. origin: *a man of noble birth.*
4. the whole it belongs to: *the handle of this spot (physical whole) \| skeletons of whales (anatomical whole) \| the innermost depths of the human soul (spatial whole; metaphor).*
5. content, domain: *the problem of the low birth rate \| the way of life \| the world of individual emotions \| by an effort of memory.*
6. possessor: *the assets of a nation.*
7. cause: *the shock of the news.*
8. composition: *a wall of stone \| a morass of complicated mathematical equations.*
9. the time it belongs to: *the winter of 1924–1925.*
10. producer: *the works of Shakespeare.*
11. apposition: *the city of Rome.*
12. relative: *the brother of John.*


### functioning in a VP
1. origin: *he was born of a wealthy family.*
2. ingredient: *that house is made of brick.*
