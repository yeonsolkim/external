### functioning in an NP
1. spatial container: *some water in the glass.*
2. temporal container: *exercising in the morning.*
3. the state something is in: *a girl in love.*
4. the group someone belongs to: *a member in the committee.*

### functioning in a VP
1. spatial background: *he studied in the room.*
2. temporal background: *Einstein provided empirical evidence for the atomic theory in 1905.*
3. manner: *he answered in English.*
4. cause: *she stepped back in surprise.*

### functioning as the complement of an AdjP
1. domain: *the country is rich in natural resources \| he was weak in math.*
