### 1. Overview
* We call a part of a sentence a *constituent*.
* A constituent has a particular *function* in the larger constituent.

### 2. Constituents
1. Lexeme
	* Verb
	   * Lexical verb
	   * Auxiliary verb
	* Noun
	* Determinative
	* Adjective
	* Adverb
	* Preposition
	* Subordinator
	* Coordinator
2. Phrase (whose head is a lexeme)
   * Verb phrase
   * Noun phrase
   * Determinative phrase
   * Adjective phrase
   * Adverbial phrase
   * Preposition phrase
1. Clause
2. Coordination

### 3. Functions
1. In a coordination: coordinate, marker
2. In a clause: predicate (VP), dependent
3. In a phrase: head, dependent

### 4. Dependents of a clause
1. Subject
2. Adjunct: an optional dependent which is not required by the predicate.
3. Marker

### 5. Dependents of a phrase
1. Complement
    * Object: a person or thing different from the subject.
       * Direct object
       * Indirect object
     * Predicative complement
2. Adjunct: an optional dependent which is not required by the head.
    * Modifier: fully integrated, attached
    * Supplement: loosely attached
3. Determiner
