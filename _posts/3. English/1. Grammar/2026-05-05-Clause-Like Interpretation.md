### 1. NPs
**1.1. Event nouns.** walk, trip, conversation, fight, arrival, meeting, decision, attempt, etc.
   > *The enemy's <u>destruction</u> of the city → The enemy destroyed the city.*
   > *I always remember a <u>mountain walk</u> with some friends → I walked in the mountains with some friends.*
   > *My <u>attempt</u> to apply this scheme to the hydrogen atom → I attempted to apply this scheme to the hydrogen atom.*

  
**1.2. State nouns.** relationship, marriage, alliance, rivalry; anger, pleasure, etc.
   > *John’s <u>friendship</u> with Mary surprised everyone → John was friends with Mary.*
   > *Her <u>anger</u> at the decision → She was angry at the decision.*


### 2. Object with Predicative Complements
> *I found <u>myself</u> <u>in an impenetrable morass</u> → Myself was in an impenetrable morass.*
> *To put <u>oneself</u> back <u>in the position</u> → Oneself is in the position.*
