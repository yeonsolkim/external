**Exercise 1.** Let $(M,d)$ be a metric space, and let $A\subseteq M$. 
1. Prove that $A'$ is closed.
2. Prove that $A'=\left(\overline A \right)'$.
3. Prove or disprove that $A'=\left(A' \right)'$.
