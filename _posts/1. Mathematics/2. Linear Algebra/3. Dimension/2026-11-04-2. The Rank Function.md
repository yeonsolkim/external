**Definition 3.2.1.** Let $V$ be a $n$-dimensional vector space. The *rank function* on $V$ is the function

$$ r:\mathcal P(V)\to\{0,1,\ldots,n\} $$

defined by

$$ r(S)=\dim\operatorname{span}(S) $$

for every subset $S\subseteq V$. The number $r(S)$ is called the *rank* of $S$.
