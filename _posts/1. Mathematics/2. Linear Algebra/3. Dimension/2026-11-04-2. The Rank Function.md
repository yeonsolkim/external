**Definition 3.2.1.** Let $V$ be a $n$-dimensional vector space. The *rank function* on $V$ is the function

$$ r:\mathcal P(V)\to\{0,1,\ldots,n\} $$

defined by

$$ r(S)=\dim\operatorname{span}(S) $$

for every subset $S\subseteq V$. The number $r(S)$ is called the *rank* of $S$.


**Theorem.** Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space. Then $\operatorname{Sub}(V)$, ordered by inclusion, is a graded modular lattice, with

$$ U\wedge W=U\cap W \quad \text{and} \quad  U\vee W=U+W. $$

Its rank function is given by

$$ \rho(U)=\dim U. $$

**Corollary.** Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space, and let $U,W$ be subspaces of $V$. Then

$$ \dim(U+W)+\dim(U\cap W) =\dim U+\dim W.$$
