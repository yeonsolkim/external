**Definition 4.1.1.** A *category* $\mathscr C$ consists of: 
1. a collection $\operatorname {ob}(\mathscr C)$ of *objects*; 
2. for each $A,B\in \operatorname {ob}(\mathscr C),$ a *hom-set* $\mathscr C(A,B)$ of *morphisms* from $A$ to $B$;
3. for each $A,B,C\in \operatorname {ob}(\mathscr C),$ a function $\mathscr C(B,C) \times \mathscr C(A,B)\to \mathscr C(A,C)$ that maps $(g,f)$ to $g\circ f,$ called *composition*;
4. for each $A\in \operatorname {ob}(\mathscr C),$ a morphism $1_A$ of $\mathscr C(A,A),$ called the *identity* on $A,$ satisfying the following axioms: 
   - associativity: for each $f\in \mathscr C(A,B), g\in \mathscr C(B,C),$ and $h\in \mathscr C(C,D)$
